# .si registry rules

Research completed 2026-10-12 from public Register.si and IANA sources. Each
rule below records the source URL and access date. The classifier is a local
pre-filter: `ok` means only “passes known rules”; it does not establish that a
domain is available or registrable at the time of a later check.

## Syntax and length

- A label directly under `.si` must contain at least 2 characters. One-character
  ASCII labels and one-character IDNs are not allowed. For an IDN, the minimum
  is measured on the Unicode label: the rules give `č.si` as disallowed even
  though its ACE form is `xn--bea.si`. Source: [General terms, clauses 4.1 and
  4.2](https://www.register.si/en/general-terms/) (accessed 2026-10-12).
- The maximum length is 63 characters. For IDNs, the maximum applies to the
  ASCII-compatible encoding (ACE/A-label), not just the Unicode label. Source:
  [General terms, clause 4.2](https://www.register.si/en/general-terms/) and
  [registration guidance](https://www.register.si/en/tips/registration-and-return/)
  (both accessed 2026-10-12).
- The first and last characters cannot be hyphens. Two consecutive hyphens in
  positions 3 and 4 are prohibited, except for an ACE string representing an
  allowed IDN. Source: [General terms, clauses 4.3 and
  4.4](https://www.register.si/en/general-terms/) (accessed 2026-10-12).
- ASCII syntax uses letters A-Z (case-insensitive), digits 0-9, and hyphen.
  The classifier canonicalizes ASCII letters to lowercase. Source: [General
  terms, clause 2.1](https://www.register.si/en/general-terms/) (accessed
  2026-10-12).

## IDN and ACE characters

IDNs are permitted. The General terms define an IDN as containing at least one
character other than ASCII `a-z`, drawn from the allowed-character table. The
table at [Allowed characters](https://www.register.si/dovoljeni-znaki/)
(accessed 2026-10-12) lists:

- Slovenian letters: `a b c č d e f g h i j k l m n o p r s š t u v z ž`.
- English letters `a-z` and digits `0-9`.
- Latin-1 Supplement: `à á â ã ä å æ ç è é ê ë ì í î ï ð ñ ò ó ô õ ö ø ù ú û ü
  ý Þ ÿ`.
- Latin Extended-A: `ā ă ą ć ĉ ċ č ď đ ē ĕ ė ę ě ĝ ğ ġ ģ ĥ ħ ĩ ī ĭ į ı ĵ ķ ĺ ļ
  ľ ŀ ł ń ņ ň ŉ ŋ ō ŏ ő œ ŕ ŗ ř ś ŝ š ť ŧ ũ ū ŭ ů ű ų ŵ ŷ ź ż ž`.
- Latin Extended-B: `ș ț`.
- Hyphen, subject to the position rules above.

The published table includes uppercase `Þ`; the classifier accepts the listed
Latin characters, normalizes accepted Unicode input to NFC, and lowercases
only explicit one-character mappings. It does not use multi-character case
folding or accept other scripts. Sources: [General terms, clauses 2.1-2.4 and
4.1-4.4](https://www.register.si/en/general-terms/) and [Allowed
characters](https://www.register.si/dovoljeni-znaki/) (accessed 2026-10-12).

The General terms define an ACE string as an ASCII string beginning with
`xn--`, generated by conversion of an IDN. The classifier accepts such labels
only when the punycode decodes to at least two characters from the published
table and the complete ACE label meets the 63-character limit. It also accepts
the Unicode form and converts it to ACE for registry requests. This project
uses the RDAP service as an ASCII-only interface and sends only ASCII A-labels
to the configured
[`rdap.register.si/domain/`](https://rdap.register.si/domain/) endpoint; no live
RDAP request was made during this task. Source for ACE handling: [General
terms, clauses 2.3-2.4 and 4.1-4.4](https://www.register.si/en/general-terms/)
(accessed 2026-10-12); endpoint is also listed in the [IANA .si delegation
record](https://www.iana.org/domains/root/db/si.html) (accessed 2026-10-12).

## Reserved names

The official [Reserved domain names](https://www.register.si/en/reserved-domain-names/)
page (accessed 2026-10-12) lists these names, which the local CSV classifies as
`reserved`:

| Label | Published reason |
| --- | --- |
| `112` | Common emergency telephone number |
| `113` | Police emergency telephone number |
| `rs` | Reserved for the Republic of Slovenia |
| `si` | Reserved for the Republic of Slovenia |
| `xeroxdiscoverysupernode1` | Forbidden due to a vulnerability in Xerox printers |
| `xeroxdiscoverysupernode2` | Forbidden due to a vulnerability in Xerox printers |
| `xeroxdiscoverysupernode3` | Forbidden due to a vulnerability in Xerox printers |

The General terms additionally describe the purposes of `112.si`, `113.si`,
`si.si`, and `rs.si` in clause 4.5. I found no `co.si`-style reserved
second-level category in the sources reviewed. The terms define a domain name
as one registered directly under `.si`; this does not make every unlisted label
available. Sources: [Reserved domain names](https://www.register.si/en/reserved-domain-names/)
and [General terms, clauses 2.2 and 4.5](https://www.register.si/en/general-terms/)
(accessed 2026-10-12).

## Restricted names

The General terms require an application to be made in good faith, for lawful
purposes, without encroaching on third-party rights, and require the domain
name not to breach law, public order, or morals (clauses 8.1.3-8.1.4). The
Registry may refuse registration or delete a name when those obligations are
not met (clause 8.2). I found no public list of restricted words, no
municipality/state-body documentation rule, and no general pre-registration
proof requirement in the sources reviewed. The published reserved list above
is recorded as reserved, not extrapolated into a larger restricted list.
Accordingly, `data/restricted_names.csv` contains only its header. These legal
conditions can require human review and are not fully decidable by a word-list
classifier. Source: [General terms, clauses 8.1-8.3](https://www.register.si/en/general-terms/)
(accessed 2026-10-12).

## Premium pricing

**Premium: no premium tier found in public sources.** The published [Tariff for
.si TLD domain name registration services](https://www.register.si/en/tariff-for-registrars/)
(accessed 2026-10-12) lists a single registry registration/renewal fee per
year (published as EUR 10 excluding VAT, valid from 2023-01-01) and no
label-dependent or premium tier. It says the fees are for registrars and that
Arnes does not set the prices registrars charge domain holders. This is
evidence about the published registry tariff, not a claim that all registrars
charge the same retail price.

## Eligibility and registration path

The General terms define a domain holder as a natural or legal person (clause
2.7) and do not state a Slovenian-residency or local-presence requirement for
holders. No holder local-presence requirement was found in the sources
reviewed. This conclusion is limited to the published terms: applications are
submitted through a registrar, and that registrar may have its own customer
requirements. Sources: [General terms, clauses 2.7, 3.5, and
9.4](https://www.register.si/en/general-terms/) (accessed 2026-10-12).

Registration applications are submitted through registrars (General terms
clauses 3.5 and 9.4). The current official list is linked from [List of
registrars](https://www.register.si/en/tips/list-of-registrars/) and appears
at [Register.si/registrars](https://www.register.si/en/registrars/) (accessed
2026-10-12). Registrar qualification is separate from holder eligibility:
Register.si says a registrar must be an EU-located business, or, if outside the
EU, ICANN-accredited, and must meet its other onboarding requirements. Source:
[Become a registrar](https://www.register.si/en/registrars/become-a-registrar/)
(accessed 2026-10-12).

IANA identifies ARNES as the `.si` ccTLD manager and lists Register.si and the
RDAP server as registration-service endpoints. Source: [IANA .si delegation
record](https://www.iana.org/domains/root/db/si.html) (accessed 2026-10-12).

## Register.si availability lookup

The official [Register.si English homepage](https://www.register.si/en/)
(accessed 2026-10-12) presents a domain input and Submit button. Opening the
public page did not show an anti-bot challenge. No lookup was submitted, so
whether submitting a query triggers a challenge was not established. No
challenge was bypassed; if a lookup requires bypassing anti-bot controls, this
project will not do it.
