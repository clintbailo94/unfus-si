How the score works

Brandability 25%; commercial 20%; shortness 15%; hack 15%; resale 15%; trademark 10%. Each part is 0-100; the total is their weighted average.

Brandability / 25%

Length, how easy it is to say and spell, letter shape and vowel balance.

Commercial / 20%

Demand for the root words (e.g. data, pay, ai) and how many kinds of buyer they appeal to.

Shortness / 15%

Length, syllables and letter variety; short pronounceable names score highest.

Domain hack / 15%

How well ".si" completes a real word, as in newsagen.si -> "newsagency".

Resale / 15%

A heuristic for memorability, root demand and rarity, not a price estimate.

Trademark / 10%

40 when the US screen found no obvious match or hasn't run yet; 25 when it found a possible conflict.
