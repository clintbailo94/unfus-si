Domain Name Wire reviewed 35 NameBio-reported `.si` sales from 2026-01-01 through 2026-06-09 and identified only Recursive.si and Military.si as apparent end-user purchases; it noted many other names pointed to resale landers and a small number of buyers accounted for a large share of activity. These pages invite inquiries and describe acquisition; they are `asking_price`, not completed sales. The `build.si` page is especially instructive because NameBio also reports a completed USD 17,435 sale of that same domain on 2026-06-02. ## Valuation implications and limits

These sources supply real `.si` transaction evidence across two-letter, three-letter, four-letter, five-letter, and longer English labels, plus a small number of AI/technology words. I found no public completed `.si` sale in this pass with the shortlist's specific “label + `.si` completes the seed phrase” word-hack pattern, and no sale of a four-letter random pronounceable label directly comparable to names such as `cizo.si` or `fuve.si`. The word and sector sales above are context, not substitutes for those missing comparisons.

The `.si` records themselves show sharply different prices by semantics, buyer, venue, and timing. The September surge also makes post-catalyst figures especially unstable. A `.com` price should not be converted mechanically into a `.si` price: extension familiarity and resale liquidity differ. With no close matched comps, a numeric speculative range for the present shortlist is not justified by these findings alone; an `insufficiently supported` result is more defensible than extrapolation.
