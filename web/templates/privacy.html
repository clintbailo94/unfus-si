Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 2026

1. Information We Collect

We collect minimal personal data required to deliver the digital service: your email address during checkout, transaction identifiers from payment processing, and standard server access logs (including IP addresses for security and download verification).

2. Payment Processing

Payment information is processed securely through Stripe. We do not store or process raw debit/credit card numbers or full cardholder details on our servers.

3. Use of Personal Information

Your email address is used solely to associate your paid license, deliver order receipts, and provide access tokens for file downloads. If you subscribe to continuous intelligence, we use your email to provide notifications of newly generated snapshot updates.

4. Data Retention and Security

Customer records and download audit records are stored in encrypted databases. We do not sell, rent, or trade your personal data to third parties.

5. Contact

For data privacy inquiries or requests to delete your customer records, contact our privacy officer via the account support portal.
